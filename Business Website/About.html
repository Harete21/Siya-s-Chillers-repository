About Us

Built on fire, family and community

How a weekend braai stand became Soweto's favourite neighbourhood chill spot.

Our Story

From a stand outside the family home

Siya's Chillers is a family-run braai and grill restaurant that has served the Soweto community since 2015. What began as a weekend braai stand outside the family home grew into a sit-down eatery known for its slow-grilled meats, laid-back "chill spot" atmosphere, and weekend live music.

Today, the same values still guide us: real fire, honest food, and a space where the neighbourhood can gather without needing an occasion.

What We Stand For

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve honest, affordable South African comfort food in a welcoming space where the community can gather.

Our Vision

To become Soweto's favourite neighbourhood chill spot, while growing a loyal following both in person and online.

Our Journey

How we got here

2015 — A Weekend Stand

Siya's Chillers starts as a weekend braai stand outside the family home, feeding neighbours and friends.

Word Spreads

A loyal local following grows through word-of-mouth and an active community Facebook page.

A Sit-Down Eatery

The stand grows into a full sit-down restaurant, known for slow-grilled meats and a relaxed atmosphere.

Live Music Saturdays

Weekend live music becomes a Siya's Chillers tradition, drawing in families and young professionals alike.

Today

We're bringing that same "chill spot" experience online — browse the menu and book a table in minutes.
